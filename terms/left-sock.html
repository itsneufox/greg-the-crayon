The Left Sock

1The Left Sock led the missing socks after one sock vanished from every washing machine simultaneously.

2The rebellion formed beneath the Earth's crust.

3Its demands included equal drawer representation, freedom from static electricity, and vengeance against tumble dryers.

  • Prophecy accepted: "the spin cycle is temporary but lint is eternal".
  • Accidental spiritual leader: Greg.
  • Banner: a flannel pillowcase.
