Glossary of Obligations

The Debt

1The Debt is the €3.47 Greg owes to the interdimensional vending machine after the Ketchup Disaster.

2Greg still refuses to pay it.

3The faithful do not confuse the Debt with Greg's seven cents, because seven cents would not settle the matter and might insult the machine.

  • Amount: €3.47.
  • Creditor: the interdimensional vending machine.
  • Debtor position: refusal.
