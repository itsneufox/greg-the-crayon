On the Moral Texture of Lint

1On the Moral Texture of Lint is the surviving dissertation from Greg's Philosopher Era.

2It is the only known academic work to argue that lint possesses ethical weight, drawer memory, and a faint contempt for certainty.

3No examiner has returned from reading it.

  • Attributed author: Greg the Crayon.
  • Academic status: banned, unreadable, technically still under review.
  • Known effect: prolonged exposure may cause ravioli-adjacent thoughts.
