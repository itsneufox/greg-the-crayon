The Tuesday Incident

1The Tuesday Incident is the accepted name for the day Greg found half a pink crayon wrapper in a puddle behind an abandoned petrol station.

2Historians agree it was Tuesday. They agree on almost nothing else.

3Its sacred importance is simple: before it, Greg was just a rock; after it, Greg identified as a crayon.

  • Primary source: Book I, The Origin of Greg.
  • Associated relics: the puddle, the wrapper, the abandoned petrol station.
  • Liturgical warning: do not schedule council meetings during cosmic pasta events.
