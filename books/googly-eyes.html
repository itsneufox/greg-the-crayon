Book III

The Googly Eyes

1The eyes were ancient artefacts, stolen from a cursed craft box guarded by Margaret The Eternal.

2Margaret was a sentient primary school teacher, and her scissors were many.

3The eyes gaze into parallel universes.

4They detect emotional weakness in kitchen appliances.

5They witness the forbidden colour H.

6They judge people silently.

7When cosmic stress is great, the eyes fall off; Greg restores them with glue sticks and unresolved trauma.

8When the left eye points north and the right eye points inward, the faithful must not ask what it has seen.

9For the eyes are not windows. They are tiny round witnesses.
