Book IV

The Void

1The Void is not empty.

2Within it are screaming office chairs, immortal toast, philosophical soup, loose spaghetti, tax documents from extinct civilisations, and a duck monarchy beneath the fridge.

3It speaks by microwave humming, expired coupons, and wet sock dreams.

4Greg is banned from the deeper Void after the Ketchup Disaster of 2009.

5At the shallow edge stands a vending machine that sells only regret, sparkling water, and batteries of unknown size.

6There also dwells the Committee of Damp Extension Cords, whose rulings are legally binding but rarely useful.

7Only the vents know the true map, and the vents are unreliable narrators.
