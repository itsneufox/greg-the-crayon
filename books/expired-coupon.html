Book XI

The Parable of the Expired Coupon

1A coupon came unto Greg, saying, "I am worth fifty cents, but only yesterday."

2Greg answered, "then you are prophecy, for all value arrives slightly too late."

3The coupon folded itself into a small paper hat and was worn by an ant with legal training.

4From this the faithful learn that expiry is not the end, but a customer-service dispute with time.
