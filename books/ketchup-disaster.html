Book V

The Ketchup Disaster

1Nobody fully understands what happened, and those who claim otherwise are asked to stand farther from the condiments.

2The official records were eaten by a possum with diplomatic immunity.

3Surviving witnesses describe red rain, a screaming vending machine, gravity temporarily becoming "sideways", and Greg yelling "THE CONDIMENTS KNOW".

4Afterwards, three dimensions folded inward, a banana achieved consciousness, and Greg owed €3.47 to an interdimensional vending machine.

5He still refuses to pay it, and the refusal has become a small doctrine of its own.

6The ketchup flowed uphill for forty-three minutes, and all spoons in the district faced east.

7Greg denied responsibility, saying only, "the bottle chose compression".
