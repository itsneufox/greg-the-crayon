Book VI

The Toaster Council

1The Toaster Council governs reality badly, and reality bears the marks of their minutes.

2They meet every Thursday inside an abandoned laundrette hidden between timelines.

3Their goals are unclear, but they deeply distrust Greg because he once microwaved bread.

4They also remember that he called a toaster "just an aggressive cupboard" and tried teaching existentialism to kitchen appliances.

5The council attempted to imprison him during the Great Sock Inquiry.

6Greg escaped using butter, and the butter was troubled but effective.

7The council speaks in crumbs and lever clicks, and its verdicts are printed on toast no one ordered.

8Greg keeps a burnt corner of their first summons as a bookmark.
