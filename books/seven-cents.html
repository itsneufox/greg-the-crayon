The Seven Cents

1Greg possesses seven cents, though no realm agrees which currency they belong to.

2One cent is lucky, one is cursed, one is asleep, and one is technically a button.

3The remaining three refuse to be counted unless addressed formally.

4Greg offers none of them to the vending machine, for the Debt is €3.47 and seven cents would only make the machine smug.
