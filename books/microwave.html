Book X

The Gospel of the Microwave

1The microwave behind which Greg first rested was not holy at first.

2It became holy after humming in B-flat for seven minutes and reheating nothing into something that looked almost like soup.

3The faithful call this the Warmth Without Plate.

4Those who press the button marked "defrost" without intention must sit quietly and consider their choices.

5Greg once tried to bless a fork in the microwave, and the fork rejected both blessing and physics.
