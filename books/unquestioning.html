Book II

The Articles of Unquestioning

1For he had googly eyes, and the witnesses beheld them, and their objections became as dust.

2Half a pink crayon wrapper was upon him, and the wrapper was sufficient vestment.

3The Toaster Council was distracted by the spaghetti eclipse and filed no objection.

4Also, the kettle had taken minutes poorly, and no minutes were accepted.

5Thus was Greg the Crayon entered into canon by accident, silence, and stationery.
