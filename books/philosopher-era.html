The Philosopher Era

1At some point Greg became a philosopher. Not a good one.

2"time is just soup wearing a watch"

3"the chair fears the ceiling"

4"every fridge contains one secret too many"

5"rock. crayon. chaos. order. whatever."

6Universities banned his work after several students dissolved into ravioli during lectures.

7Yet one dissertation survived, titled On the Moral Texture of Lint, and no examiner has returned from reading it.
