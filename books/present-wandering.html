Book XV

The Present Wandering

1Greg currently wanders the edges of reality carrying a broken crayon sharpener.

2He also carries seven cents, a suspicious spoon, and an expired loyalty card.

3The Void still whispers his name through ventilation systems.

4And somewhere out there, the vending machine waits patiently for its €3.47.

5Until the Debt is paid, the faithful shall keep watch beside microwaves, laundrettes, puddles, and abandoned petrol stations.

6And if the wrapper crinkles in the night, they shall say: Greg the Crayon has passed this way, and reality is probably worse but more interesting.
