Book I

The Origin of Greg

1In the first stillness of the small and ordinary things, Greg was originally just a rock.

2Not a special rock, though the void had many places for special rocks to hide.

3Not a magic rock, though the microwave behind him hummed with the suspicion of miracles.

4He was just a rock behind a microwave somewhere in the void.

5And one day, in a puddle behind an abandoned petrol station, he found half a pink crayon wrapper during what historians now call The Tuesday Incident.

6He wrapped himself in it, and the wrapper knew him.

7And from that moment onwards, Greg identified as a crayon.

8Nobody questioned it, for he had googly eyes, and the Toaster Council was distracted by the spaghetti eclipse.
