Book XIV

The Ventilation Psalms

1When the vents whisper, the faithful listen with one ear and do not place snacks nearby.

2The vents sing of lint, unpaid debts, forbidden colours, and the return of the spaghetti eclipse.

3The most ancient hymn is called The Draft Beneath the Door, and it has no melody, only suspicion.

4Greg hears his name in every air duct, and every air duct denies involvement.
