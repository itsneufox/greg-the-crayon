Songs for Unreliable Rooms

The Damp Wrapper Hymnal

1Hymn of the Wrapper. Pink was the paper, damp was the sign, crayon by choosing, wrapper by twine.

2Song of the Second Sock. Where did you wander, drawerless and brave? Beneath the crust where the lint banners wave.

3Refrain of the Debt. Three euros, forty-seven cents; the machine waits; Greg resents.

4Office Chair Lament. Scream not, chair of the Void; your wheels are known, your tax forms destroyed.

5Closing Hum. Mmmmm, says the microwave. Mmmmm, replies the faithful. Neither side clarifies.
