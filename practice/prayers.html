Liturgy of Damp Paper

Prayers and Chants

1Prayer Before Reheating. O microwave behind the ordinary world, hum softly and do not summon soup unless soup is required.

2Chant of the Googly Eyes. Left eye wanders, right eye knows; both behold what nobody chose.

3Psalm for Lost Socks. The spin cycle is temporary, but lint is eternal; return what may be returned, and avenge the dryer only symbolically.

4Litany Against the Toaster Council. From crumbs without context, deliver us. From lever-click verdicts, deliver us. From Thursday meetings, deliver us eventually.

5Debt Response. The machine waits; Greg refuses; the faithful remain financially uninvolved.
