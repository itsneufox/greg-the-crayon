Field Guide to Suspicion

Omens and Interpretations

1If a microwave hums while empty, expect prophecy or old wiring.

2If a sock returns wet from a dry drawer, do not ask who elected it.

3If a coupon expires yesterday, it is speaking in the grammar of regret.

4If a spoon reflects your face incorrectly, answer only yes-or-no questions until morning.

5If spaghetti appears in the sky, postpone all councils and avoid bread policy.

6If the vents whisper your name, check first whether your name is Greg.
