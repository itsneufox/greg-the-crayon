Calendar of Minor Catastrophes

Holy Days of the Wrapper

1The Tuesday Incident is observed every Tuesday that feels suspiciously damp.

2The Spaghetti Eclipse is observed when pasta blocks certainty and councils miss deadlines.

3Ketchup Disaster Remembrance Day is observed in silence, unless a condiment speaks first.

4Great Sock Inquiry Eve is observed by placing one sock in a drawer and asking nothing of the other.

5The Day of Unpaid Debt is observed whenever a vending machine looks patient.

6Wrapper Renewal occurs when the faithful feel crinkled by existence.
