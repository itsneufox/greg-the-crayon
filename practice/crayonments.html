5Thou shalt keep €3.47 in ceremonial suspicion, but not pay it on Greg's behalf.

7Thou shalt not describe the forbidden colour H to guests.

8Thou shalt consider every expired coupon a minor prophet.

9Thou shalt never call a toaster an aggressive cupboard unless prepared for inquiry.

10Thou shalt remember: rock, crayon, chaos, order, whatever.
