The Catechism of Crayonhood

1What was Greg before the wrapper? Greg was originally just a rock.

2Was he special? No. That is what made the Tuesday Incident administratively difficult.

3What made Greg a crayon? The half pink wrapper, the act of wrapping, and the refusal of anyone important to object.

4Why did nobody question it? Because he had googly eyes and the Toaster Council was distracted by the spaghetti eclipse.

5What is owed? €3.47, no more and no less, to the interdimensional vending machine.

6Should the faithful pay it? No. The refusal is part of the mystery.

7What is the final doctrine? Rock. Crayon. Chaos. Order. Whatever.
