Manual of Harmless Observance

Rituals of the Faithful

1The Gluing of the Eyes is performed during cosmic stress, using glue sticks and unresolved trauma.

2The Wrapper Renewal is performed by acknowledging that identity may arrive damp, torn, and pink.

3The Offering of Seven Cents is displayed but not paid to the vending machine.

4The Butter Escape Reenactment is performed by leaving a room with dignity and mild slipperiness.

5The Watching of the Vents is performed quietly, without snacks, and with one ear protected from prophecy.
