Marginal Writings of Dubious Discount

The Coupon Apocrypha

1The Coupon Apocrypha contains sayings found on receipts, loyalty cards, and the backs of vending-machine complaints.

2"All value arrives late, but some value arrives laminated."

3"A discount denied is still a prophecy folded wrong."

4"Beware the ant with legal training, for it has read the terms."

5"If the offer excludes batteries of unknown size, the Void has already chosen."

6The church permits these writings, but does not let them near the accounts.
