Roll of Questionable Persons

Saints, Heretics, and Prophets

1Margaret The Eternal, guardian of the cursed craft box, is feared but acknowledged as necessary to the googly-eye mystery.

2The Left Sock, rebel beneath the Earth's crust, is honoured as a prophet of drawer representation.

3The Conscious Banana, awakened after the Ketchup Disaster, is listed as a witness but refuses interviews.

4The Possum With Diplomatic Immunity, eater of official records, is considered a heretic by archivists and a saint by shredders.

5The Pineapple With a Monocle is remembered for surviving nineteen hours of soup-regret debate without conceding.

6The Vending Machine is not a saint, but all saints are advised to avoid eye contact with it.
