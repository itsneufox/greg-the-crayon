Pilgrimage Sites

1The Microwave Behind Somewhere. Pilgrims stand respectfully behind any microwave and consider the dignity of ordinary rocks.

2The Abandoned Petrol Station. Not all petrol stations are the one, but the faithful know to look for puddles that seem historically nervous.

3The Laundrette Between Timelines. Visitors should not attend on Thursdays, when the Toaster Council meets and misgoverns reality.

4The Edge of the Void. Pilgrims may listen for microwave humming but must not enter the deeper parts on Greg's behalf.

5The Vending Machine. Pilgrims should keep distance, avoid coins, and never insert regret unless clearly instructed.
